What comes with a holiday dinner?

Each dinner is a package: one meat, one holiday vegetable, one holiday side, a bread and a salad. The Holiday Ham is glazed and spiral cut. The Smoked Turkey also comes with dressing, gravy and cranberry sauce.

Vegetables include Green Bean Casserole and Glazed Carrots. Sides include Traditional Cornbread Dressing, Mashed Potatoes with Gravy and Mac & Cheese. Breads include Cheese Biscuits and Hawaiian Sweet Rolls.

The arithmetic for 12 guests: 12 x $18.95 = $227.40. An extra Sweet Potato Casserole for all 12 at $3.50 adds 12 x $3.50 = $42.00, which makes $269.40 in food.

How big are the finger-food orders?

Finger foods are sold by the dozen, with a 3-dozen minimum order. Three dozen Meatballs is 3 x $24.99 = $74.97. These hold up on a table for a few hours while guests come and go, which suits an open house. For the $3.50 Banana Pudding and Peach Cobbler, ask how a portion is sized.

How does ordering work?

Orders take 24 hours' notice, and the same day can work when the schedule allows. December fills up, so book earlier for a Friday lunch or a Saturday party.

Have the headcount, date, delivery address and any allergies ready when you call. Hot meals come with plates, napkins, cutlery and serving utensils. Events get chafing dishes set up on site.

Gluten-free hot entrées are marked GF on the printed menu. Vegetarian dishes such as Veggie Lasagna and Stuffed Portabella Mushrooms are on the regular menu. If a dish is not listed, ask.

FAQ

Where does Rx Catering NWA deliver? It delivers to Fayetteville, Springdale, Rogers, Bentonville and the towns around them, plus Fort Smith, Van Buren and Harrison. Drop-offs go to an office, home, church hall or venue.

Can a Christmas dinner serve fewer than 10 people? The dinner packages carry a 10-person minimum. For a smaller table, call and ask what the kitchen can arrange.
